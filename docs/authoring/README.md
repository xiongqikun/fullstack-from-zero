# Authoring kit

> 这个文件夹不是课文，你不需要读。它是建课的“工艺说明书”：后面每一个阶段的课，都按这里写好的同一套流程来建、来测，而不是靠谁记得。准备下一阶段时（你对导师说“准备阶段 2”），导师和建课的 AI 从这里开始。

This folder makes "the same quality process" (design section 5.9) a set of files instead of a memory. It is for whoever builds or repairs lessons: a builder or tester agent, the tutor in its course-maintenance role, or a human. The binding documents stay the plan (`docs/plans/2026-11-19-batch-1-plan.md`, section 1 above all) and the design (`docs/design/2026-11-19-fullstack-learning-workspace-design.md`); the files here apply them.

## The order of work for a new stage

Each step is described in [`how-to-generate-the-next-stage.md`](how-to-generate-the-next-stage.md); the step names below are its section titles.

1. **Stage review from the learner model.** Read `learner/state.json`, `learner/log.jsonl`, `learner/profile.md`, `learner/misconceptions.md`, the journal, the stage retrospective and the errata in `CHANGELOG.md`, and answer the questions of `docs/curriculum-spine.md` section 8.2 with numbers. Re-verify the facts that age fast.
2. **A one-page design the learner confirms.** Goal, product increment, lessons and hours, what changes in the way of learning, the decisions that are the learner's (topic, pace, electives). The tutor shows it; nothing is built before the learner confirms it.
3. **Build.** Extend the plan and `tools/courselib/syllabus.py`; one builder per lesson with `build-kit/build-lessons.js`, every command executed in a simulation home ([`tool-api.md`](tool-api.md) section 17.1), against [`lesson-template.md`](lesson-template.md).
4. **Blind test.** A different agent with only the lesson folder and the tool follows the text literally and injects the novice errors; fix, then confirm. Then audit the seams between lessons and repair them (`build-kit/fix-lessons-round.js`), write the shared files (`build-kit/build-shared-docs.js`) and accept the whole (`build-kit/acceptance.js`) against [`definition-of-done.md`](definition-of-done.md).
5. **Delivery in small batches.** Four or five lessons per batch; the learner studies the first one before the rest is finished, and what was unclear or slow changes the next ones.
6. **Changelog.** One `CHANGELOG.md` entry per batch: what was added, what changed elsewhere, which facts were re-verified and when; the hours in `ROADMAP.md` and the stage page recomputed (plan G5a).

## `build-kit/`

The scripts are kept as they ran, as the record of the process and as the starting point for the next stage: `build-lessons.js` (with `lessons.json`) and `fix-lessons-round.js` as in the build and repair rounds of 2026-11-19 (the repair round ran on into the night of 2026-11-20); `acceptance.js`, `build-shared-docs.js` and `tutor-tests.js` in their present form, which is the form in which they ran on 2026-11-20. They are workflow scripts for Claude Code's multi-agent workflows: each one starts agents with a prompt and a JSON schema for the report (described in [`qa-report-format.md`](qa-report-format.md)), and takes its inputs as `args`. They contain the absolute paths and briefing texts of their run: copy one, adapt its prompts and arguments to the new stage, and keep the copy next to the build records.

| File | What it does | Arguments |
|---|---|---|
| `build-lessons.js` | Plan task T2. Per lesson: a builder writes `lesson.md`, `lesson.py` and `rules.md` and executes every command in a simulation home; an independent blind tester follows the text and injects novice errors; a fixer applies the findings; a second test confirms lessons that had a blocker. Phases: Write, Blind test, Fix, Confirm | `ws`, `scratch`, `research`, `lessons` (the content of `lessons.json`), `extra`, `model`, `effort` |
| `lessons.json` | The input of `build-lessons.js` for the 20 lessons of stages 0-1: per lesson `id`, `stage_dir`, `folder`, `title`, `briefs` (the research topics its builder read) and `notes` (the lesson-specific brief) | |
| `fix-lessons-round.js` | A repair round over lessons that exist: per lesson a fixer works from a briefing folder (one `COMMON.md` of binding decisions plus one `<lesson id>.md` with the last test, the seam findings and the tool changes), a fresh blind tester re-tests, and the pair repeats while blockers or majors remain | `ws`, `scratch`, `briefs` (the briefing folder), `lessons`, `rounds`, `extra`, `model`, `effort` |
| `COMMON.md` | Not a script: the binding decisions of 2026-11-19 that `fix-lessons-round.js` gave every fixer, with the rulings of 2026-11-20 at the end. Kept as the record that `lesson-template.md`, `tool-api.md` and the comments in `tools/` cite as "COMMON section N"; its content is folded into those files | |
| `build-shared-docs.js` | Plan task T3, after the lessons exist: single writers on disjoint files (the reference assembler, the entrance `README.md`, the tutor rules, the kit of cheat sheets and this folder, `STANDARDS.md`); then checks (the tutor rules tested by simulation in fresh contexts and scored by a judge; mechanical, consistency and newcomer reviews; auditors of the seams between lessons), fixers with disjoint files, an independent confirmation of the fixes, and a finalize step that regenerates the references, validates and runs the tests | `ws`, `scratch`, `research`, `today`, `stage` (`write`, `check` or `all`), `only` (which writers), `writers` (earlier writers' reports), `lessons` and `groups` (which fixer owns which lessons, or `per-lesson`), `seams_r1` (findings of an earlier audit of the seams), `extra_findings`, `extra_dir` and `extra_counts` (findings carried over from earlier steps), `snapshot` (a copy of the workspace from before the fixers), `concurrency`, `notes`, `model`, `effort` |
| `acceptance.js` | Plan task T4: five play-throughs of the course in order, each in one simulation home with novice-error injection, then fixes, an independent confirmation, and the mechanical acceptance checklist with the QA record | `ws`, `scratch`, `today`, `notes`, `model`, `effort` |
| `tutor-tests.js` | Tests the tutor rules (`CLAUDE.md` and `docs/tutor/playbook.md`) against the situations of [`tutor-tests.md`](tutor-tests.md): each situation staged in its own simulation home with real tool runs, a fresh tutor answers, another agent judges the reply against the rows; one owner changes the contract, the failed situations run again with fresh tutors, an independent agent checks every change. Phases: Kit, Round 1, Fix, Round 2, Confirm | `ws`, `scratch`, `today`, `situations` (the ids to run, for example `["S12", "S15", "S26"]`), `carried` (a file of items carried over to the contract's owner), `helper_ready` (true: `stage_state.py` exists, skip the Kit phase), `concurrency` (default 4), `notes`, `model`, `effort` |
| `stage_state.py` | The helper of `tutor-tests.js`: writes a simulated learner's state through the course tool's own code (real `check`, `hint`, `status`, `exam`, `journal` and review; only the verdict of a check or task is chosen). Run with `HOME` set to a simulation home and from inside its workspace copy; it refuses otherwise. Start the script of the real workspace, not the copy inside the simulation: it refuses to stage the workspace it belongs to. Python 3.9 or newer, standard library only | `apply <spec.json \| -> [--verbose]` (stage the steps of a spec), `show [--on YYYY-MM-DD]`, `peek --now <date> [--keep] [--interactive] -- <tool arguments>` (run the tool with that `COURSE_NOW`, then restore the learner's files unless `--keep`), `--self-test [--workspace <dir>]`, `--help-spec` (the spec format with examples) |

Related tools outside this folder, in `tools/dev/`: `make_sim_home.py` builds a simulation home that mirrors the reference environment (plan G7, G8); `build_starter_manifest.py` records the original starter files (plan G11a) and must be run with `--write` after any change under `starter/`; `build_reference.py` (added by the writer of the shared references) regenerates `handbook/` and `GLOSSARY.md` from the lessons' `rules.md` cards and `TERMS`, and `--check` reports when they are out of date; `export_public.py` copies the course without the learner's records into a new folder for publishing and checks it (plan G10a), and `--check` checks the workspace itself.

## Rules that do not change between stages

- Plan section 1 binds every file: purpose (G1), voice (G2), the two labels of every rule (G3), freshness (G4), honest minutes (G5a), verification marks (G6), the reference environment (G7) and simulation parity (G8), safety (G9), privacy (G10), publishable (G10a: no facts about one learner or their computer in course files, every case public or plainly invented), ownership (G11, G11a), naming (G12), the English ladder (G14), the three practice places (G15).
- Builders and testers commit nothing in the learner's workspace, install nothing globally, create no accounts and push nothing (plan G9).
- Facts come from the machine or from a primary source opened that day, never from memory. Where this kit and a fact verified today disagree, the fact wins and this kit is corrected, with a `CHANGELOG.md` line.
